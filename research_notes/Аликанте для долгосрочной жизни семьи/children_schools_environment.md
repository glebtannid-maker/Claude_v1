## 1. Which candidate schools exist and are current: screening of about 12 names, and the 5–7-option shortlist

### Takeaway
Of the names on the brief, 7 relevant, currently operating full-day schools were confirmed within a reasonable distance of Alicante: the European School (ESA), King's College, El Valle Alicante, the English School of Mutxamel, Newton College (Elche), and the Fomento schools Altozano and Aitana. Two names in the brief are misleading. "King's College Alicante" and "British School of Alicante" are **the same school**. "Aitana International College" does not appear to exist: the Torrellano school is **Colegio de Fomento Aitana**, a Catholic **boys-only concertado**. El Limonar (Villamartín), Villa de Altea and Lady Elizabeth are real but too far away for daily trips from Alicante. No data was found on Lope de Vega (Benidorm).

### Inferences
- **Suggested shortlist** for a family living in Playa de San Juan, Cabo de las Huertas, Albufereta or Vistahermosa, ranked by fit with the goals of an international environment and strong English:
  1. **ESA (European School).** The most international and multilingual option. The risks are that Category III places are limited, the language section follows the child's dominant language, and applications open once a year. The 2026/27 window has already been missed.
  2. **King's College, The British School of Alicante.** A full British school in Alicante, with current fee figures and a published nationality mix.
  3. **Colegio El Valle Alicante.** The closest private school to Playa de San Juan. It is bilingual and claims the full IB continuum (unverified). Mid-range cost.
  4. **Newton College (Elche).** Strong international and IB-type profile. Cost is lower than King's, but the school is in Elche.
  5. **The English School of Mutxamel.** British curriculum, but very little data was found.
  6. **Budget or integration option:** a public CEIP with the plurilingual programme (free). See section 3.
  7. **Conditional option:** Fomento **Altozano** (girls) or **Aitana** (boys). Concertado, cheap, and strong English hours, but single-sex, with a Catholic ethos and mostly Spanish families. This is a weak match for the family's international-environment goal.
- **Screened out:** El Limonar Villamartín, Villa de Altea, Lady Elizabeth and Lope de Vega (Benidorm). All are far from Alicante city (my estimate: roughly 40–80 km, UNVERIFIED), and nothing I found shows them to be clearly better.
- A Russian passport is not in itself a barrier at any of these schools. ESA's Category III is simply every child who is not in Category I or II (definition in section 2).

## 2. The shortlisted schools compared: language, curriculum, entry class, diplomas, 2026/27 costs, timetable, commute, international mix, accreditation, places and reviews

### Takeaway
**Full 2026/27 fees could be sourced only for King's College** (via an aggregator): **Reception €9,100 per year, lunch included**, plus a bus at €130–198 per month. **Newton College** has only 2025/26 figures (aggregator): Reception **€6,730 per year**, plus a **€1,650 one-off** registration fee and an **€825 annual** enrolment fee. **El Valle** has only a range: **€300–700 per month**. For ESA, the **Category III tuition amounts were not found**. What was found: payment runs 25% by 30 June and 75% by 30 November (2025/26 rules); children enter **Nursery in the calendar year they turn 4**; and the **language section follows the dominant language, possibly after a test**. For a child born in 2021 or 2022, the realistic ESA entry is **September 2027**, through the application window of roughly April–May 2027.

### Cited Findings
#### A. European School of Alicante (ESA)
- **Category III:** pupils who do not belong to Categories I or II. They are admitted according to available places, within the limits set by the European Schools regulations. Their families pay the **"minerval"** (tuition), which the Board of Governors fixes every year. — [ESA Category III](https://escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/category-iii); [ESA Enrolments](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/enrolments); [EURSC admission](https://www.eursc.eu/en/European-Schools/enrolments/admission)
- The Board of Governors has exclusive power over fees, and any change it decides overrides published documents. Fees for all European Schools are published on eursc.eu. Billing contact: ALI-BILLING@eursc.eu. — [EURSC school fees](https://www.eursc.eu/en/european-schools/enrolments/school-fees/); [ESA Category III 2025-2026](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/category-iii-school-year-2025-2026)
- **2026/27 procedure (official):**
  - The application window was **17 April – 8 May 2026**.
  - Families had to read the **Enrolment Policy 2026-2027** and a **circular on class availability**, which says whether each year and language section is open to Category III applications.
  - Applications were made through an online form.
  - **25% of the annual tuition had to be paid before 30 June 2026** to confirm the place. It is **non-refundable** and payable **only by bank transfer**.
  - — [ESA Categoría III 2026-2027](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/escuela-europea-de-alicante/categoria-iii-ano-escolar-2026-2027)
- **Rules from the 2025/26 documents:** a **€200 administrative fee** for a Category III application; 25% paid up front, with the remaining **75% due by 30 November**; instalment options available. The search extract did not pin down which of the two pages each rule comes from. — [ESA Enrolment policy 2025/2026 (PDF, Feb-2025)](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/sites/default/files/2025-02/ESA_Enrolment%20policy%202025_2026.pdf); [ESA Category III 2025-2026](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/category-iii-school-year-2025-2026)
- **Age at entry:** "Pupils shall be admitted to the Nursery school at the beginning of the school year in September of the calendar year in which the child reaches 4 years of age." — [ESA Enrolment policy](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/sites/default/files/2025-02/ESA_Enrolment%20policy%202025_2026.pdf); [ESA Enrolments](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/enrolments)
- **Language section:**
  - The application must be for the section matching the child's **mother tongue or dominant language**.
  - The school management may organise **comparative language tests, "even for enrolment in the nursery cycle"**.
  - Under the General Rules, a pupil whose mother tongue has no section (for example Russian) should in principle be enrolled in the **French, English or German** section.
  - — [ESA Language sections](https://escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/language-sections); [ESA Enrolments](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/enrolments)
- **Small extra costs for Nursery** (2024/25–2025/26 documents): school insurance **€5.50**, printing **€15**, school diary **€9.10**. — [ESA Circular, beginning of school year 2025-2026 (PDF, Jul-2025)](https://www.escuelaeuropea.org/sites/default/files/2025-07/Circular%20beginning%20school%20year%202025_2026.pdf); [ESA Common expenses 2024-2025](https://escuelaeuropea.org/en/escuela-europea-de-alicante/common-expenses-and-general-information-school-year-2024-2025)
- **Reviews (secondary):** "perhaps the most expensive in the province, but the students leave with 3 languages spoken perfectly". Good facilities, and a focus on well-being and creative learning. — [ischooladvisor](https://www.ischooladvisor.com/spain/alicante/alicante/european-school-alicante)

### Inferences
- **Entry class for a child born in 2021 or 2022** (the calendar-year grouping is the standard Spanish rule, UNVERIFIED in this session; the ESA Nursery rule is sourced above):

| School type | Born 2021: 2026/27 | Born 2021: 2027/28 | Born 2022: 2026/27 | Born 2022: 2027/28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish system (CEIP, concertado, El Valle) | Infantil 5 años (3º EI) | 1º Primaria | Infantil 4 años (2º EI) | Infantil 5 años |
| British school in Spain (King's, Mutxamel)* | Year 1 | Year 2 | Reception | Year 1 |
| ESA** | Nursery 2 | Primary 1 | Nursery 1 | Nursery 2 |

\* This assumes the usual Spanish equivalence for British schools in Spain, where Year 2 corresponds to 1º Primaria. Confirm with admissions.
\*\* ESA places for 2026/27 are closed, so the realistic entry is 2027/28: **P1 for a child born in 2021** and **N2 for a child born in 2022**. Primary 1 in the calendar year the child turns 6 is the European Schools norm, not verified in this session.

- **ESA and a Russian-speaking child:** a place in the **English** section is not guaranteed. The school allocates sections by dominant language and may test even at Nursery level. Because Russian has no section, the child would in principle go into the English, French or German section. The family should build up the child's English before the spring 2027 application.
  - ESA ends with the **European Baccalaureate** (European Schools norm, UNVERIFIED in this session).
  - Fees are paid by bank transfer, so a Spanish or EU bank account will be needed in practice.
- **King's College, cost with lunch and bus** (Reception figure labelled 2026/27):
  - Tuition alone is €9,100 per year, which is **about €758 per month over 12 months** (€910 over 10 months).
  - Adding the bus at €1,300–1,980 per year gives **€10,400–11,080 per year, about €870–925 per month**.
  - This excludes the registration or enrolment fee (not found), uniform, and after-school or holiday care.
  - If doris's "2026/27" label actually holds the 2025/26 figures, the 2026/27 fee is likely a few percent higher.
  - Roughly 9% of pupils are Russian (one source). So there is a Russian-speaking peer group, but it does not dominate, and 48–52% of pupils are foreign.
- **Newton College, recurring cost:** about €7,555 per year (tuition + annual enrolment fee), **about €630 per month over 12 months**. Add €1,650 once in the first year. Lunch and bus are unknown and probably extra.
  - Elche is about 20–25 km from Playa de San Juan, so roughly 25–35 minutes by car (**my estimate, UNVERIFIED**). The TRAM does not go to Elche, so a school bus would be needed.
